The manager of communication, digital networks, and energy networks at Runjian Co., Ltd.

In the era of Internet of Everything, Runjian Shares stands as a leading provider of digital and intelligent operations & maintenance services (AIops), driven by the vision to "be the steward of the IoT, ensuring no network is ever difficult to manage." The company delivers cutting-edge digital and intelligent O&M solutions, tailored digital solutions, smart energy-saving solutions, and product integration services. Today, Runjian's business spans across critical areas such as communication networks, digital transformation, IDCs, and new energy sectors.

 

Building on its computing power and data services, the company leverages its independently developed "Qu Chi" generative AI industry model development platform to create industry-leading models, products, and solutions. These innovations empower key sectors such as communication networks, digital networks, and energy systems, driving productivity gains across industries through cutting-edge AI technology.
