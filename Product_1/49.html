Integrated Media Operations Platform

Today, the media market is becoming increasingly competitive, with traditional media facing a massive challenge from emerging platforms. There is an urgent need for these established players to transform and upgrade their strategies in order to enhance their competitive edge. Meanwhile, in 2022, the National Radio and Television Administration emphasized in its document "Media Convergence: A Major Initiative Requiring Both Focused Efforts and Long-Term Commitment" that China's media sector must continuously align with the "China Plan" for integrated development of the media industry, as outlined by the Party Central Committee with Comrade Xi Jinping at its core—specifically as articulated during the 18th National Congress of the Communist Party of China.
Product Introduction
The integrated media platform adopts an open architecture featuring "platform + tools + services," providing an operational support system for the next-generation, platform-based media model that combines "information, operations, and services." It offers media organizations a one-stop solution for content gathering, editing, production, distribution, and management—while also helping governments, universities, and enterprises successfully build their own integrated media ecosystems.
