Digital Energy
RunDoBidder Virtual Power Plant
In recent years, the installed capacity of new energy sources such as photovoltaics and wind power has grown rapidly, and the development and application of non-fossil energy are steadily gaining momentum. To maintain grid balance and stability while boosting the integration of renewable energy, various types of virtual power plants have emerged swiftly across China. As a unique type of power plant, virtual power plants serve as a coordinated management system that seamlessly integrates into electricity markets and grid operations. They can function both as "positive power plants," reducing electricity consumption to help ease peak demand on the grid, and as "negative power plants," increasing consumption to fill valleys in electricity usage. Capable of responding swiftly to grid signals, virtual power plants not only help stabilize the power system and earn financial incentives but also enable participation in diverse electricity market activities—such as capacity trading, spot markets, and ancillary services—thereby unlocking significant economic benefits.
