Green Computing Power × Token Factory — ASEAN Green Intelligent Computing and AI Innovation Ecosystem Conference Held Successfully at WAIC 2026

2026-07-27 16:18

Source:

Author:

On July 18, during WAIC 2026 (World Artificial Intelligence Conference), the “Green Computing Power × Token Factory — ASEAN Green Intelligent Computing and AI Innovation Ecosystem Conference” was successfully held at the WAIC venue.

The conference was guided by the Office of the World Artificial Intelligence Conference Organizing Committee, Nanning Municipal People’s Government, Cyberspace Administration of Guangxi Zhuang Autonomous Region, China Council for the Promotion of International Trade Guangxi Sub-council, Secretariat of the China-ASEAN Business and Investment Summit, Guangxi Big Data Development Bureau, China-ASEAN Artificial Intelligence Application Cooperation Center, Guangxi Department of Commerce, Foreign Affairs Office of Guangxi Zhuang Autonomous Region, Guangxi Association for Science and Technology, among other organizations.

The event was hosted by Runjian Co., Ltd. and Wuxiang Cloud Valley, organized by Entrepreneurship China (CYZone), Shenzhen Artificial Intelligence Industry Association, and Guangxi Artificial Intelligence Industry Alliance, with support from Guangxi Artificial Intelligence Society and Guangxi Electronic Information Association.

 

The conference focused on the complete value chain of “Green Energy Infrastructure — Token Factory — Token Services — Token Globalization — Industrial Ecosystem”, bringing together government representatives, academicians, experts, industry leaders, and ecosystem partners from China and ASEAN countries to explore new development pathways for industries in the Token era.

During the event, several major initiatives were officially launched, including:

  • Wuxiang Cloud Valley Token Factory RONZON Platform 
  • Runjian·Zhiyu AI Security Product Series 
  • Runjian Token Sales and Service System 

Meanwhile, strategic cooperation agreements on China-ASEAN Token development and ecosystem collaboration were signed. Runjian Co., Ltd. also partnered with the China Academy of Information and Communications Technology (CAICT) to launch research and standardization initiatives for the Token Factory industry.

Through multi-party collaboration, the conference injected new momentum into China-ASEAN cooperation in the digital economy.

 

Industry Insights: Exploring New Opportunities for China-ASEAN Collaboration

As artificial intelligence enters the era of reasoning and large-scale model applications continue to accelerate, Token is emerging as a new fundamental production factor connecting computing power, AI models, and industrial applications.

Focusing on key topics including green computing infrastructure, China-ASEAN digital cooperation, and Token industry development, the conference gathered leading experts and industry representatives to discuss future opportunities.

Li Guangshao, Deputy Secretary of the Party Leadership Group of Nanning Municipal Government and Executive Deputy Director of the Management Committee of Wuxiang New Area, delivered the opening remarks. He shared Nanning’s strategic planning and development initiatives for advancing the green computing industry through Wuxiang New Area.

Yang Yanyan, President of the China Council for the Promotion of International Trade Guangxi Sub-council, Secretary-General of the China-ASEAN Business and Investment Summit Secretariat, and Secretary-General of the China-ASEAN Business Council Joint Secretariat, shared insights into Guangxi’s latest progress in promoting cross-border industrial cooperation and digital economy exchanges under the framework of China-ASEAN digital collaboration.

As the host representative, Xu Wenjie, President of Runjian Co., Ltd., delivered a keynote speech titled “Runjian’s Token Strategy and Vision.”

He introduced Runjian’s strategic framework of:

“Token as the Core, Green Computing Collaboration, Intelligent Operations Empowering Industries.”

The speech highlighted Runjian’s full-stack Token capability system covering:

  • Token Production 
  • Token Services 
  • Token Globalization 
  • Industrial Ecosystem Development 

Xu Wenjie expressed the company’s commitment to working with more industry partners to elevate China-ASEAN cooperation in artificial intelligence to a new level.

Focusing on technological advancement, Academician Ling Wen from the Chinese Academy of Engineering delivered a keynote speech titled “Integrated Engineering of Computing, Data, Energy and Networks.”

He emphasized the need to accelerate the development of a green, efficient, and secure next-generation infrastructure system to support large-scale AI applications.

Peng Shaoliang, Deputy Director of the National Supercomputing Center in Changsha at Hunan University, shared practical experiences in integrated computing and energy systems as well as global expansion of computing capabilities, with a focus on open-source AI models and intelligent agents.

Global Launch of Token Ecosystem Solutions and Products

During the product launch session, Runjian Co., Ltd. officially unveiled its comprehensive suite of platforms, products, and service systems built around the Token industry value chain, showcasing its latest innovations to the global industry community.

Deng Chongzhi, Chief Technical Expert of Shanghai Xinhong Intelligent Technology Co., Ltd. and RONZON (Nanning) Technology Co., Ltd., introduced and explained the RONZON Platform of Wuxiang Cloud Valley Token Factory, highlighting its core capabilities in Token production, scheduling, delivery, and operations.

Wu Minhua, General Manager of Shanghai Lichan Technology Co., Ltd., Dong Weijun, General Manager of Wuxiang Cloud Valley Co., Ltd., Hua Minglei, General Manager of RONZON (Nanning) Technology Co., Ltd., and Ding Yong, Director and Vice President of Runjian Co., Ltd., jointly witnessed the global launch ceremony of the RONZON Platform of Wuxiang Cloud Valley Token Factory.

The platform will further enhance Token production capabilities and operational efficiency, supporting the development of a more mature Token ecosystem.

As AI applications continue to accelerate, data security, LLM security, and agent security have become critical concerns across the industry.

In response to this trend, Runjian Co., Ltd. officially launched the “Runjian·Zhiyu” Intelligent Security Product Series at the conference.

Dr. Chu Ge, Chief AI Security Scientist of Runjian Co., Ltd., provided a systematic introduction to the capabilities and application scenarios of four key products — Hive、GateKeeper、Model Inspection and Model Shield — presenting a comprehensive security framework covering the entire Token lifecycle.

Witnessed by Chen Junhai, expert from the Cybersecurity Brigade of the Public Security Department of Guangxi Zhuang Autonomous Region; Cui Hanxin, Chief Information Officer of Guangxi Beibu Gulf Bank; Peng Lin, President of TBEA Intelligent Research Institute; Wang Jitian, Deputy General Manager of the Port Logistics Business Division of Guangxi Beibu Gulf Big Data Technology Co., Ltd.; and Liu Zhipei, Senior Security Expert at Guangdong Nanfang Information Security Research Institute, the “Runjian·Zhiyu” Intelligent Security Product Series made its global debut.

With the continuous development of the Token industry ecosystem, the establishment of comprehensive service systems has become a key driver for industry growth.

During the forum, Runjian Co., Ltd. also launched its Token Sales and Service System.

Huang Haijiang, Executive Vice President of Runjian’s TOKEN Business Group, provided an in-depth overview of the system’s service model, delivery process, and ecosystem collaboration framework, while introducing the achievements made in building the Token sales service system.

The initiative will provide strong support for future industry partnerships and market expansion.

 

Connecting ASEAN: Exploring Token Expansion Across Borders

As Chinese enterprises accelerate their global expansion, ASEAN has emerged as one of the most dynamic overseas markets for AI applications and the Token industry, driven by its rapidly growing digital economy, open business environment, and broad range of application scenarios. This has created new opportunities for deeper China–ASEAN collaboration.

To explore these opportunities, the forum featured a dedicated ASEAN Case Study Session, focusing on Token services, enterprise AI applications, and localized operations, where industry leaders shared practical insights and experiences.

 

AHMAD FUZLI BIN FUAD, Vice President of the National Aerospace & Defence Industries (NADI) of Malaysia, shared practical experience in AI applications and Token services in the ASEAN market, drawing on local industry development.

Yin Jie, General Manager of Qinghe Resonance, a subsidiary of Runjian Co., Ltd., delivered a presentation titled "Token Economy in ASEAN: Apollo11 Enterprise AI Foundation and FDE Service Deployment Guide." He showcased real-world applications of the Apollo11 Enterprise AI Foundation and FDE services across the ASEAN market.

 

 

A key highlight of the conference was the signing of two major cooperation initiatives: the China–ASEAN Token Strategic Cooperation Agreement and the Token Ecosystem Cooperation Agreement.

Representatives from industry, academia, and ecosystem partners came together to expand the Token industry collaboration network and promote deeper cooperation between China and ASEAN in artificial intelligence, digital industries, and related innovation ecosystems.

 

Inclusive Innovation: Empowering Every Industry with Token

As the Token service ecosystem continues to evolve, AI capabilities are rapidly expanding beyond foundation models into real-world business scenarios, enabling more enterprises and organizations to adopt artificial intelligence with lower barriers and greater efficiency.

The forum showcased a wide range of Token service applications across government, manufacturing, legal services, education, and cross-border commerce, demonstrating how AI is evolving from isolated pilot projects to large-scale deployment across multiple industries.

 

He Jun, Vice President of the AI Business Group at Runjian Co., Ltd., introduced the company's AI FDE Service Foundation and industry application roadmap. He presented practical examples of how Token services support the development of industry-specific AI agents and the delivery of digital transformation projects, providing a solid foundation for the large-scale adoption of AI.

As practical implementations continue to expand across industries, the application boundaries of Token services are becoming increasingly well defined. During the forum, representatives from the government, manufacturing, legal, education, and cross-border commerce sectors shared real-world AI deployment experiences and best practices.

 

Advancing Industry Standards to Build a Thriving Token Ecosystem

The forum concluded with a roundtable discussion themed "How the Token Economy is Reshaping AI Business Models and Expanding into ASEAN Markets." The session was moderated by Fan Congming, Executive Secretary-General of the AI Committee of the All-China Federation of Industry and Commerce and Special Advisor to the Guangxi Artificial Intelligence Industry Alliance.

During the discussion, distinguished guests—including Zhao Shuangliang, Vice President of Guangxi University; Liu Wenzhong, Deputy General Manager and Chief Technology Officer of Renmin Data; Professor Lau Siu Yue, Dean of the Faculty of Information and Communication Technology at Universiti Tunku Abdul Rahman (Malaysia); Liu Jian, Deputy Director of the Energy Systems Research Center at the Energy Research Institute of China's National Development and Reform Commission; Cen Huanhui, Head of Guangxi Business at Volcano Engine; and Li Shuanglei, Deputy General Manager of Wuxiang Cloud Valley Co., Ltd.—shared insights on topics including green computing infrastructure, ecosystem collaboration, AI commercialization, and China–ASEAN cooperation.

At the close of the conference, Cao Feng, Director of the Platform and Engineering Department at the Artificial Intelligence Institute of the China Academy of Information and Communications Technology (CAICT), joined representatives from the China–ASEAN Artificial Intelligence Application Cooperation Center, Guangxi Artificial Intelligence Society, Runjian Co., Ltd., Wuxiang Cloud Valley, Guangxi Beibu Gulf Bank, and other organizations to officially launch the Token Factory Industry Research and Standards Initiative.

The initiative aims to establish the first industry standards for Token services in China, filling a critical gap in the sector while promoting standardization, high-quality development, and long-term sustainability across the Token industry.

From green intelligent computing infrastructure to the Token ecosystem, and from industry applications to China–ASEAN collaboration, the forum not only showcased the latest achievements of the Wuxiang Cloud Valley Token Factory, but also reinforced its role as a strategic hub connecting AI industries across China and ASEAN.

With the approval of the Nanning International Communications Gateway Bureau, Guangxi is accelerating the development of an international data corridor serving ASEAN markets. Leveraging its strategic geographic location and the opportunities created by the New International Land-Sea Trade Corridor, Runjian Co., Ltd. will continue expanding its Token capabilities globally, working closely with industry partners to unlock new opportunities for China–ASEAN AI cooperation and inject fresh momentum into the region's digital economy.
